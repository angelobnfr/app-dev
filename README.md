## My Favorite Movie: *Toy Story 5*

**Toy Story 5** is a Pixar animated film released in theaters on June 19, 2026. It is the fifth movie in the Toy Story franchise, directed by Andrew Stanton. This time, the toys face a modern challenge: kids who would rather play with tablets and screens than with toys.

### Main Characters
- **Woody** - the loyal cowboy doll
- **Buzz Lightyear** - the space ranger
- **Jessie** - worried about losing Bonnie to technology
- **Rex**
- **Hamm**

### Why I Love It
1. The friendship and loyalty between the toys
2. The story about toys vs. technology
3. The humor and heart that work for all ages
